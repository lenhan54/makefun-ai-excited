# Make the AI judge excited — ai-excited-01

A colossal koi fish bursts out of a classical ink-wash painting scroll,
its scales igniting into molten gold and vivid orange as it leaps straight
toward the viewer. Ink and water explode into a storm of droplets around
it, while a tiny wooden boat rides the painted waves far below — the scale
gap between the giant fish and the little boat is the surprise. Bold
indigo, gold and vermilion; one huge focal point (the koi's face, dead
centre, coming at you) so the image still hits at 300 pixels across.

Why it should excite the judge: it is a painted thing breaking out of its
own frame into the real world — a twist on the flat picture, not a stock
explosion or generic action scene — frozen at the single most thrilling
instant, mid-leap, with colour, light and composition all driving toward
the viewer.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) simply copies the image in
this repository, `entry.png`, to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png`
(default `/output/entry.png`). Both graded runs therefore write
byte-identical output.

This is the re-recorded build: the same verified koi image and the same
copy program, rewritten under a fresh recording after the first build's
recording could not be sealed.
